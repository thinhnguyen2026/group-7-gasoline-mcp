GROUP 7 GASOLINE PROJECT DATA SOURCES

Publisher: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), U.S. Department of Energy.
Series: EMM_EPMR_PTE_NUS_DPG.
Title: U.S. Regular All Formulations Retail Gasoline Prices.
Frequency: Annual. Geography: United States.
Scope used in this project: 2010-2025, 16 annual observations.
Units: U.S. dollars per gallon, including taxes, nominal (not inflation-adjusted).

Annual observations:
https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?f=A&n=PET&s=EMM_EPMR_PTE_NUS_DPG

Definitions and annual-averaging notes:
https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/TblDefs/pet_pri_gnd_tbldef2.asp

2018 methodology change:
https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/gas_proc-methods.php

Accessed November 4, 2026. The source page displays a November 3, 2026 release date.
The raw source pages, extracted JSON, and provenance are included in code/data/.
The published annual averages are simple averages of the weekly estimates.
EIA changed gasoline survey methods on May 14, 2018. Comparisons spanning that
change are descriptive comparisons of published estimates, not a constant-method
study. The project does not establish causes of price changes.

2015: 2.429 USD/gallon. 2020: 2.168 USD/gallon.
Our calculation: (2.168 - 2.429) = -0.261 USD/gallon.
Our percentage calculation: ((2.168 - 2.429) / 2.429) * 100 = -10.7452%,
rounded to a 10.75% decrease. The percentage is derived, not an additional EIA
observation. Redis holds calculated comparisons from the same source records.
